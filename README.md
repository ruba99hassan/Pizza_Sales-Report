# Pizza_Sales-Report
This project analyzes pizza sales data to uncover key business insights related to revenue, customer ordering behavior, product performance, and sales trends.
The analysis was performed using SQL Server for data exploration and KPI calculations, and Power BI for data modeling, visualization, and dashboard development.

The goal of this project is to help stakeholders understand sales performance, identify top-selling products, optimize inventory planning, and improve decision-making through data-driven insights.

*Key Insights:
- A classic and superme pizza are contribute a significant portion of total sales.
- Certain hours of the day consistently generate higher order volumes, indicating peak demand periods.
- Sales performance varies by day of the week (increase in weekdays), revealing opportunities for targeted promotions.
- Some pizza categories outperform others in both revenue and quantity sold.
- A number of low-performing pizzas contribute minimal revenue and may require menu optimization.
- Larger pizza sizes generate a substantial share of overall revenue.

*Business Recommendations:
1. Optimize Inventory Planning

Focus inventory management on top-selling pizzas and ingredients to reduce stock shortages during peak demand.

2. Improve Staffing Allocation

Schedule additional staff during peak ordering hours to improve customer service and operational efficiency.

3. Promote Low-Selling Products

Create promotional offers and bundle deals for underperforming pizzas to increase their visibility and sales.

4. Increase Revenue Through Upselling

Encourage customers to upgrade to larger pizza sizes, which contribute significantly to overall revenue.
5. Target Low-Demand Days

Launch special promotions and discounts on days with lower order volumes to drive additional sales.

6. Menu Optimization

Evaluate consistently low-performing products and consider redesigning, repositioning, or removing them from the menu.
